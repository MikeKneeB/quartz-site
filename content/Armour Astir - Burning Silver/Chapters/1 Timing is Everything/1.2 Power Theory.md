# Conflict Report

As the conflict turn kicks off, the Grand Unified Mining Concern & The Protection Guild
make some initial moves. The Mining Concern needs more workers to expand its operations,
and so starts a new scheme "Further Work Conscription".

The Protection Guilds operations in the shadow has them infiltrating and placing grip on
[[The Resistance#Quake|Quake]], there are eyes everywhere and the propaganda campaign
against Quake is kicking up into high gear, especially following the events at New Volt.

## Meeting Adjourned

_An unfurling plan, with the Grand Unified Mining Concern targetting Quake._

At the top spire of the Administration Fortress in New Volt, the heads of the Town and
High Marshal Felicity (visiting them temporarily, on her way to Farsight Outpost). With
the setting sun lancing through the long, high windows looking out over the desert,
Felicity calls the meeting to order.

She is sitting in a high-backed chair, long cloak draped around her, lance in hand.
Around her sit the highest ranked Mining Concern functionaries from the Town of New Volt,
**Castle Warden Farron** and **Director Fredward Timson**. They are additionally joined
by the newly promoted **Captain Marie Susan** and Farron's Adjutant **Grillys**.

**Felicity** is grave, the prisoners were sprung, and this looks like a major failure on
the part of the Mining Concern. She rounds on the **Warden**, looking for answers. He has
none, only excuses and a tired plea to **Director Timson** for more resources to protect
the town. _The first challenge, an ask to commit more resources._

"And why should I, Warden? You take more and more from the coffers but the results, as
this morning's debacle demonstrate, are so plainly lacking."

_The challenge fails._ **Grillys** wheedles that they're doing everything they can, not
just for the director's interests but for the people of New Volt as well. **Timson** is
unmoved, and uncaring.

**Marshal Felicity** notes that the perpetrators seemed to know much of the days
proceedings, the ins and outs of the day were clearly known ahead of time. There may be a
leak in the New Volt Administration Fortress. _The second challenge, evidence of a spy in
their midst._

**Captain Susan** is adamant that such a thing is simply not possible _and the challenge
fails._

We zoom out of the highest spires of New Volt, as the room descends into arguing and
strife.

## Inciting Action

_All out war, between The Free Duchies of Nald and The Protection Guild._

Deep in the jungle, in the deep night, awash with the glow of plants and the buzz and hum
of life, a tent sits glowing with an incongruous halogen sear. Seated inside, **Victor
Ravenseye**'s moustache quivers, he surveys the war table with a look of bemused
puzzlement that looks worryingly at home as it settles over his face. He has the faux
aristocratic air of many young officers of the Protection Guild brought in from noble
families.

He picks up the cast array, and it crackles to life. Deep in the jungle, down in a dark
trench, surrounded by the grim faces of hardened warriors, the connecting sigil crackles
to life in the array held by **Major Danger**[^1].

"Ah, Major, how goes it in the trenches? Jolly good, look old chap, I reckon a move to
the left flank is just what's needed here. See it done, there's a good man."

In the trees nearby, a patch of the bark shifts slightly, an arm emerging from a
shimmering cloak the eye seems to slide off to swing a monocular up to the eye of **IIIrd
Lance Wilhelmina**. She is looking down on **Major Danger**, as her attache **Strong
Thomas** reloads her ornate Free Duchy made long-rifle.

"What do you think Lance? What's our move?" whispers Thomas, in a low hum like a sleeping
dog.

"Well, if they move out onto their left flank now we'll be in trouble, but they're
scared. Patience I think Tom."

_The first challenge, you could complete a key objective at cost, is issued by me. I tell
Major Danger's player he can see that Victor Ravenseye is actually right, a move to the
left is critical, but it is likely to cause him to lose the company of men he has holding
the centre._

**Major Danger** wipes his hand down wearily over his eyes, he's seen too much death to
lose an entire company. _Rolling, and unfortuantely failing._

From their position in the trees, **Strong Thomas** passes **Wilhelmina** her own cast
array, and she links up with her Sodality command.

"Bring the 14th Griffon's Claw to our position, they're lost in their own cowardice."

_Another challenge, I lead an unexpected ambush, which Major Danger rolls and fails
again - meaning this next scene is also a win for the Resistance._

**Major Danger** looks up into the forest, just in time to see the gigantic sword of the
**Power Theory**, pilotted by 2nd Lance Undine of the 14th Griffon's Claw, scream down
into his arrayed troops in a flash of magic and steel. The Astirs of Griffon's Claw
descend like a lightning bolt onto the lines of his troops, the jungle judders with
scattered weapons fire and broken screams of routing Protection Guild forces.

In the morning, the war continues, but the Protection Guild are pushed back, finding no
foothold tonight.

## Affirmation of Faith

Back in New Volt, **High Marshal Felicity** is in confessional with **Pastor Lavinia**.
Both of them, on opposite sides of the screen partition, make the sign of the Silver
Church - a hand over the eye. **Lavinia**, however, is a Fallen Dragon. She has long
brown curls, falling around her round rich-brown face and over her shoulders. She has
wide, well-like eyes, just on the edge of tears.

_This is a one-on-one between the Silver Church & the Fallen Dragons._

It begins with **Felicity** admonishing herself, she has failed, in her eyes, and in the
eyes of the dragons. The rebels should never have been allowed to escape. **Lavinia** is
taken aback, but behind the partition composes herself, and readies herself with a
whispered prayer.

"Perhaps, High Marshal, there is another way? Have you forgotten, that the dragons way is
peace and plenty for all mortals. We are eager to deal out judgement, to ourselves and
others, but perhaps temperance would serve us better. The violence dealt out cannot be
placed solely at the Rebels feet, without the Mining Concerns iron grip over the
citizenry these Rebels would have no cause to fight."

_The first challenge, my guard seems to drop for a moment, is succeeded by Lavinia_.

**Felicity** falters, and in her side of the confessional, closes her eyes and exhales.
**Lavinia**'s words hit down onto something deep within her, some core of doubt about her
mission. Her shimmering purpose falters, a crack snaking across the mirror polish of her
steely faith and fortifying catechisms. Is there, as **Lavinia** says, another way.

As she steps out of the confessional she calls back,

"Would you pray with me a moment, Lavinia?"

_The second challenge, I push you and our fight to a new location, is rolled and failed
by Lavinia._

**Lavinia** leads them both in an old prayer of the faith, one of the founding ones. It
is potentially filled with ever so slightly more brimstone and fire than the situation
called for. Perhaps she wanted to allay any suspicions that she might have called down
onto herself by her words in the booth.

**Felicity** sighs with relief, "My thanks, Lavinia, these older prayers always
solidifies my faith when I waver."

She stands, with a sweep of her cape, taking up her lance from where it rests against the
confessional.

"Walk with me. We must redouble our efforts, Lavinia, you are right there may be other
ways. But I leave them with you, there are many teeth in the dragon's maw. I will
continue to serve as I am best suited, as the sharpened point of the couched lance.

"New Volt clearly hides something, I will talk with the Warden. The Rebels must have some
network here, despite the protestations of the incompetents in the fortress. We will
drive them out, Lavinia, for the unity and prosperity of all mortals."

She whirls out the open doors of the Church, leaving Lavinia standing in her wake, her
smile faltering in privacy to a scowl. She has failed to dissuade Felicity from a path of
blood and flames, but at the very least she has clearly germinated a seed of doubt in the
deep depths of her grim counterpart's heart.

_The final challenge, I make an attack that threatens innocents, is succeeded by tapping
The Assembly for a success._

Lavinia turns back into the shadows of the church. Later in the dead of night, she is
stalking the Undercity, in the deep shadows of a dim bar, she meets with an Assembly
member, relaying in hushed tones Felicity's intentions for the city of new volt. This is
passed to **JilMil**, who makes preparations to leave town, organising the other
Resistance sympathisers and dissidents in a whirl of frenetic activity.

The scene ends with Lavinia standing her ground, risking her position to pass this
critical information back the Resistance, and undeniably saving countless lives.

Later, back in the same workshop, [[The Pillar of Indignity#Aubrey Gene, The

- Lab or workshop led by Aubrey, starting a long-term project to use her Astir move more

Finally the whole crew meets, at [[The Pillar of Indignity#Lily 523, The Whither|Lily's]]
insistence, on the bridge with **Solomon Za** and the rescued [[The
Resistance#Quake|Quake]] cell to brief for the upcoming mission.

The bridge is a wide sweeping room, the briefing table on a raised platform above the
consoles and controls of the sand-ship itself. Huge forward-angled windows look out over
the desert rushing past, and the sound of the crew running the ship hums in the
background.

**Solomon Za** calls the meeting to order and invites **Jessie J. Needle** to share what
they know. She nods to her fellow [[The Resistance#Quake|Quake]] operative, **Nord
Arndor**. **Nord** is a heavy set man, with a bald head and close beard. He wears a long,
plain robe, well-worn by travel. His deep brown eyes roll back into his head as he
extends a hand a summons a shimmering image from his outstretched palm onto the briefing
table.

The magical projection renders the surroundings of the Farsight Outpost in gut-swimming
detail, the vision of a high-flying hawk brought down into miniature. Within the
almost-solid vision the crew can see:

- The Farsight Outpost lurks like an infection in a long crevasse deep in a dense region
  of jungle.
- One side of the crevasse is higher than the other, and atop the higher side are
  brutally functional obelisk perches, with a huge crackling orb of energy floating just
  above it.
- There is a [[The Argent Court#The Grand Unified Mining Concern|G.U.M.C.]] mine to the
  south-west, at the base of a long ragged scree-slope where there was probably once
  jungle.
- South of that is a dedicated mining settlement.

**Jessie J.** begins to chatter excitdely,

"Ok, look, here it is: Farsight. Down here in the crevasse, see. Above it, the Javelin
Engine. Nasty piece of magic, takes ten channelers a day to keep it operational, and they
don't last long at that post let me tell you, takes it out of them I hear. Pretty much a
nightmare for anything unauthorised (that's you lot by the way, deeply unauthorised, us
as well y'know nothing personal), 'vapourised before they could scream' is what I heard.

"So tough break right? Pack up go home? Not on your life.

"We got a hold of some prett-ay nice intel, the Gummies operation here is chugging along
happy as Larry, but not always looking at their charts as carefully as they might.
They've dug a tunnel out into a pretty large underground cavern, lucky break they
thought, until they realised that same cave was part of deep-storage for Farsight. Proper
cock-up for gummy leadership, egg on faces (probably shoulders too).

"So, what do they do? Well, in classic court fashion no-one wants to admit they're wrong
so they're just keeping shtum about it. Hastily sealed up the hole, but no major work
it's just a foot or so of rubble between the mine and one of the most clandestine
Protection fortresses.

"That right there is your in, through the mine and into the newly renovated backdoor
courtesy of Grand Unified."

The crew asks some further clarifying questions, but this seems like a good entrance.
They opt to move up through the Jungle, then down towards the mine from above it, as
coming fast down the slope to the mine from above is likely to be less well-fortified.
