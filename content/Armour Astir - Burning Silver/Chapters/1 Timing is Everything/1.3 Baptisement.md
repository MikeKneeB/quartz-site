# Mission Report

# Setup

## What's Happening

The new AGLA war automata is being finalised at the underground **Farsight Outpost** just
outside the contested borders of
[[The Resistance#The Free Duchies of Nald|The Free Duchies of Nald]].

Marshal Felicity is overseeing the final preparations, and has delivered the baptisement
core, provided by the [[The Argent Court#The Silver Church|Silver Church's]] techno-magic
artificers. She is travelling on her fortress-island **Faultless Fury**.

A [[The Resistance#The Free Duchies of Nald|Free Duchies]] ranger coterie have attempted
infiltration previously, but are MIA in the jungles around **Farsight**.

## Locations

### A - Lower Jungle

The desert ends abrubtly, the sand giving way to a wall of dense trees without ceremony.
It's a long trek towards Farsight.

Find here:

- Jungle sounds.
- Strange critters and weird plants.

### B - Hound 3 Extraction Camp

The Hound 3 extraction camp is a collection of shabby, quickly assembled stone and cloth
dwellings. It is dominated by an imposing metal structure - the Hound 3 operations
fortress.

Find here:

- Overworked miners.
- A dingy mess-hall.
- A company shop.
- An illegitimate speakeasy.
- The Hound 3 Operations centre - actually a mobile mini-fortress.
  - Hound 3 Operations Centre IV (MUNDANE)
    - Crushing legs IV (Melee)
    - Defensive cannons IV (Ranged / Messy)
    - Sheet plating (+ward, Mundane attacks are made in desperation)
- Various GUMC personnel & Ardents as required.

### C - Mineshafts

The entrances to the Hound 3 mine are large openings that cut into the shallow slope of
the ragged cliff leading up to the jungle. They are surrounded by a wide staging area,
filled with various bits of heavy mining equipment, worker Ardents, and buildings. There
is also a train station, and tracks leading in from the west.

The whole area is enclosed by a semicircular defensive wall, that meets up with the
shallow cliff. The wall is dotted with towers, and an imposing gatehouse squats over the
traintracks and main road leading to the Camp.

The mine is currently shut, due to mole problems.

Find here:

- Overworked miners.
- Lots of mining equipment.
- Various GUMC personnel & Ardents as required.
- **Captain Marie Susan** and **Director Timson**, overseeing mining operations.
- A "slacker" strung up in a gibbet, caught trying to organise a workers mutiny and
  escape to join the Shifted Sands.

### D - Deep Jungle

The deep jungle is close and hot. Lit by filtered light from far above the canopy and the
eery bioluminescent glow of many plants, it has a strange twilight atmosphere throughout
the day.

Ranger Coterie The Griffon's 4th Eye, led by **IIIrd Lance Wilhelmina** are hiding around
the ruins of the wreckage of a Protection Guild mounted squadron. If approaching the
wreckage, _Weather the Storm_ to spot them before they spot you.

Find here:

- Jungle sounds.
- Strange creatures and weird plants.
- Large and dangerous predators.
- The ruins of a Protection Guild mounted squadron.
- The Griffon's 4th Eye, led by **IIIrd Lance Wilhelmina**.

### E - Farsight Outpost

Farsight outpost sits deep in a crevasse. One side of the gorge is higher than the other,
the entrance to farsight is on the lower side.

The entrance leads to a switchback path down into the crevasse, at the base is the
Farsight Outpost proper. It is a huge complex, built in the bottom of the crevasse and
dug into the cliff to the south-west.

A large elevator shaft shoots up from the complex under the higher cliff-face, leading to
the Javelin engine & the flight dock platform.

Find here:

- A lot of Protection Guild personnel & Ardents.
- **Princeps Forte Lure** (Side: Rigid, Eager)
  - Pilots the **Moral Vacuum** III (PROFANE)
- Deep storage of arms and armour for the war against The Free Duchies.

### F - The Farsight Javelin Engine

The Farsight Javelin Engine lurks at the top of the cliff, looking down with a commanding
view of both the farsight facility.

The Javelin Engine is accessed by a lift from the Farsight Outpost.

Find here:

- The Javelin Engine V (DIVINE)
  - Javelin Drive (Sniper / Ruin, Heaven-sent, Obliterating)
- 11 javelin channelers, hooked up directly to the drive in the main core.
- The flight-dock, built into the cliff just below the Javelin Engine.
- Various Protection Guild personnel & Ardents.

### G - Hound 3 Mine

Dark mine-shafts, the techno-magic torches off while the mine is out-of-commission.
Abandoned mining Ardents are left powered-down.

Some of the tunnels don't appear to be quite as regular as the others, rather they look
like they might have been made by huge scraping claw-marks.

Find here:

- Abandoned mining Ardents.
- Giant, pissed-off moles[^1].
- A mole queen, nesting deep in the tunnels.

### H - Deep Caves

The deep caves open out into a huge underground ravine, with a rushing river down at the
base. The GUMC have set up a heavy-duty bridge over the ravine.

On the other side, a hastily refilled mine shaft entrance can be seen.

Find here:

- The sound of fast-rushing water, echoing from the depths.
- A sturdy bridge.
- A roughly-blocked passage leading to the deep storage halls of Farsight Outpost.

## NPCs

### The Protection Guild

- Princeps Forte Lure (side: calm, dedicated)
  - Paired revolvers (ranged / blitz)
  - _Astir_: Moral Vacuum III PROFANE
    - Giant paired revolers (ranged / blitz)
    - Shadow step ritual: Once per scene, can teleport a moderate distance

### Silver Church

- High-Keeper Jory Ten (side: persistent, indiscriminate)
  - _Astir_: Perfect Purpose III DIVINE
    - Giant greataxe (melee / area, impact)
    - Jump drive (blitz)
    - Speed of the dragons: once per scene this astir can close the distance quickly with
      someone.

- Candidate 00 (main: FURY, FIRE, HATE) DIVINE
  - _Automata_: Prototype AGLA 00 IV
    - Sword array (melee / blitz, defensive)
    - AGLA Baptism Engine: As the AGLA engages in combat, the armour plates on it petal
      open and begin to vent heat. Actions against the automata take a flat penalty equal
      to the number of dangers it has. At 2 dangers, dragon blood leaks from the open
      armour and its approach changes to PROFANE.

### G.U.M.C.

- Captian Marie Susan (side: eager, out of her depth)
  - _Astir_: Drillbit III ELEMENTAL
    - Longsword (melee)
    - Greatshield (ward, defensive)
    - Loud-hailer, allies weapons get Bane when nearby.
- Director Timson (main)
  - _Armoured Train_: Wheels of Progress IV MUNDANE
    - Heavy armour (ward)
    - Cannon array (ranged / area, suppressing)
    - Tracked: Can't move apart from on train tracks.

### Other

- Mole II (extra: furious) ELEMENTAL
  - Powerful bite (melee / pinning)
  - Tunnel creature: Counts as one tier higher when fighting in tunnels, mines, or caves.

- Mole Queen IV (side: furious, hungry) ELEMENTAL
  - Sweeping tail (melee / area)
  - Huge maw (melee / restraining)
  - Brood call: while the Mole Queen is alive, it can summon a Mole to its side each time
    a hostile fails a roll in its presence (in addition to any other consequences they
    might suffer).

[^1]:
    As we all know, a mole is a 7' to the shoulder lizard with six limbs. The front two
    have huge shovel-like digging claws, the back 4 are stumpy but powerful. It has a
    long, arrowhead-shaped head with small and almost entirely vestigial eyes set on top.
    It has a set of four long antenna-like whiskers sprouting from above its jaw. Its
    mouth is wide, stretching all around its flat head. Inside are rows of vicious teeth,
    at the very front a pair of rodent-like incisors meet in a vicious plier-like pinch.
