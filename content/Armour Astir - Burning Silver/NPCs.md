# G.U.M.C.

G.U.M.C. ardents & astirs are all bulky and heavy-set. They tend to have fairly angular
armour plating, and often a pair of exhaust chimneys coming out their back. They are
often painted in greens and browns. They tend to be ARCANE (drawing on the power of the
formulate), MUNDANE (drawing on the power of gunpowder and sharp edges), and rarely
ELEMENTAL (drawing on the power of the earth).

Type names: \[-ence/-ance word\] Pattern

- GUMC Security personnel (Extra: brutal) (ARCANE)
  - Arc-baton (melee) I
  - _Ardent_: Compliance Pattern II
    - Loadout: Town Guard
      - Saw stave (melee / messy)
      - Flux thrower (ranged / messy)
    - _The compliance pattern is a heavy, imposing ardent that would look right at home
      in a mineshaft, but is more commonly found patrolling the streets of mining towns.
      It has wide legs covered in heavy armour, and huge round shields on each shoulder.
      It's head has an aspect like a hounskull bascinet._

- GUMC Elite (Extra: single-minded) (ELEMENTAL)
  - Assault Glaive (melee / bane) I
  - _Ardent_: Obediance Pattern III
    - Loadout: Elite Guard
      - Giant Earth-drill (melee)
        - When this ardent is attacked with a ranged or sniper weapon it can burrow into
          the ground, and rapidly close the distance with its attacker.
        - When this ardent bursts from the earth, the next roll against it is made with
          disadvantage.
      - Kite Shield (+ward)
    - _The Obediance pattern is like an up-scaled version of the Compliance. It has lost
      the circular shoulder protection, in favour of more armour across the board. They
      are typically equipped with huge drills, which have inscribed runes that let it
      swim through the earth like water._

      _The head retains the hounskull shape, but gains a large metal crest._

- GUMC Artillery Specialist (Extra: cautious) (ARCANE)
  - Standard-issue cry-pistol (ranged) I
  - _Ardent_: Influence Pattern II
    - Always has:
      - Spindly legs: good at getting over difficult terrain, but slow and fragile.
      - Hopeless close-combatant: always act with confidence against the Influence in
        melee range.
    - Loadout: Bombardier
      - Ice Flux Bombard (sniper / area, bane, lobbed, setup)
    - Loadout: Lance
      - Freeze-thaw lance (ranged / ruin, piercing, unwieldy)
    - _The Influence pattern is mostly just a big gun on six legs. Those legs are long
      and slender , pretty good at getting them into interesting places, but are neither
      fast nor particularly well-armoured._

      _The body without a gun looks sort of line a crane top without the arm, the cockpit
      is to one side of a flat bed on which the gun sits on an artillery-like swivel
      platform._

- GUMC Defender (Extra: stubborn) (MUNDANE)
  - Sword & Shield (melee / +ward) I
  - _Ardent_: Adherence Pattern II
    - Always has:
      - Heavy armour: Melee attacks are made at disadvantage against the Adherence, but
        the ardent is slow and cumbersome.
    - Loadout: Engager
      - Harpoon cannon (ranged / harpoon) Can force a roll to engage an ally to be made
        at disadvantage, if the roll fails the target cannot move away from the Adherence
        until they _Weather the Storm_ to free themselves.
      - Warpick (melee)
    - Loadout: Shield-wall
      - Partisan (melee / reach)
      - Tower shield (+ward) ranged and sniper attacks are made at disadvantage against
        the Adherence.
    - _The adherence pattern is larger than the compliance, it has a huge rounded torso,
      sitting atop two wide legs, flared out towards the feet. It has a sallet-like
      helmet, recessed in to the chest slightly._

# Protection Guild

The Protection Guild Ardents & Astirs tend to be sleek, with fluted rounded armour
plating, painted black. They tend to be PROFANE or ELEMENTAL (water) and occasionally
DIVINE.

- Protection Guild Operative (Extra: sneaky) PROFANE
  - Silent death-hex (ranged / silent) I
  - _Ardent_: Kopesh Category III
    - Always has:
      - Blink drive - can teleport
    - Loadout: assassin
      - Dual curved swords (melee)
      - Binding rituals (restraining)
    - Loadout: sniper
      - Death's eye bow (sniper / set-up, guided, bane)
      - Ice knives (ranged / defensive) ELEMENTAL

- Protection Guild Heavy (Extra: brutal) ELEMENTAL
  - Tower shield & pike (melee / defensive) I
  - _Ardent_: Claymore Category III
    - Always has:
      - Wave drive - once per scene can push or pull someone away or toward them, forcing
        them to `weather the storm` or be moved and take disadvantage on their next
        action
    - Loadout: Enforcer
      - Greatsword & tower shield (melee / area, defensive, ward)
    - Loadout: Cannoneer
      - Tsunami cannon (ranged / reload, area) - if you fail or partially succeed a roll
        against an ardent armed with this weapon, it can choose to push you away

- Protection Guild Fast Attack Squad II (Extra: savage) PROFANE
  - Razor knives (melee / bane)
  - Always has:
    - Squad tactics - operate as a squad
    - Jump jets (blitz) - Can fly short distances.
  - Loadout: Spotters
    - Target acquisition rituals - once per scene can upgrade a risk an ally applies to a
      peril.

# Silver Church

The Silver Church Ardents & Astirs tend to have very flowing, almost flame-like armour
plating. They are richly adorned, and very often have a cape or a helmet plume. They tend
to be DIVINE or ELEMENTAL (fire), and occasionally harness the ARCANE.

  - _Ardent_: Sceptre Class II
    - Always Has:
      - Retribution of the dragons: After receiving a danger from any actor, that actor
        acts in desperation against the Sceptre.
    - Loadout: Antecedent
      - Great Spear (melee / anti-charge)
      - Holy Rifle (ranged / guided)
- Dragon Acolyte (Extra: fervent) (ELEMENTAL)

  - _Ardent_: Robe Class II
    - Always Has:

        acts in desperation next time it rolls.
    - Loadout: Torch
      - Great fire-pike (melee / area, bane)
    - Loadout: Breacher
      - Blazing axe (melee / bane)
      - Breacher shield (defensive)
