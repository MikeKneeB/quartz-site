Rumours of strife at the Manse of Mirrors have not gone unnoticed by certain
opportunistic ears. The Grand Wizard Zoldar would very much like to get his sparkly
wizard fingers on the legendary Eye of Aeons and he will certainly spare as little
expense as possible to do so.

# Study in Purple

The camera pans down from a chandelier that is really trying it on in the
ostentatiousness department, revealing a sumptuous office absolutely full to bursting
with the kind of useless tat that Wizards seem to really like. It smells sharply of
brimstone and dried blood, but obviously that doesn't come through as, like I said, this
is all on film.

Seated in front of the huge leather-topped desk, on chairs very obviously made smaller
than necessary, are five down on their luck adventurers:

- **Stickman**, a Gremlin Catcher with a flat cap, boots, and spurs
- **Bungle** a Poorly-Made Downwarf covered in corrections and helpful notes drawn onto
  his body by other dwarves (everyone's a critic)
- **Greg** a foul-smelling Befouler of Ponds
- **GBPAR11** a Thinking Engine and ex-golden barge lifeboat pilot, joined by their two
  hands **Metatron** and **Lucifer**
- and finally **Lola** the Cacogen, a black veiled hunched crone-like figure, ex-golden
  barge _actual_ pilot (she crashed the last one)

Zoldar breathes out a cloud of woody purple smoke from a pipe,

"Right, so that pretty much sums it up: get in, grab me the eye, get out. I'll pay you
1000 Silver if you do."

"Can we have it in advance?"

Camera fades to black to the sound of Zoldar's uproarious hacking laughter.

# Fighting Fire

We rejoin our fated adventurers outside the Manse of Mirrors, the huge stone edifice in
front of them looms meancingly. They approach, and climb the massive stairs toward the
grand entrance, and the main foyer.

Inside, surrounded by the wreck and ruin of a brutal battle, they begin to cautiously
investigate. Zoldar said they'd "know it when they saw it" but was pretty light on
details about what the eye actually looked like and where it might be found.

Looking through the first door to the west, they see it blocked by a mountain of odds and
ends, discarded furniture and crockery and the detritus of what seems to be many many
years. There seems to be no real call to try and scramble over just yet, so onto the next
door.

Behind that is a long corridor with many doors leading off it. **Lola** can hear a cry
for help coming from further down the corridor, and the gang set off to investigate. They
pin down the door, but find it locked[^1] . Investigating the surrounding rooms yields no
potential other ingress, so **Bungle** hefts his axe and chops vigorously into the wood,
opening a small hole[^2].

**GBPAR11** sends in a hand to investigate, and it skitters through the hole into the
room, only to skitter very hurriedly back out again as an _Alzabo_ lunges and attempts to
crush it with a massive paw. The cries of help turn to a guttural roaring scream and the
creature lunges for the door and snaps its fleshless jaw through the opening.

With the door now structurally weakened, the Alzabo begins to attack it with some vigour,
and the whole gang can tell that it's going to break in fairly short order. To mitigate
this, **Lola** attempts to cast Flash through the opening, but the Alzabo seems unfazed.
**Bungle** has an alternative idea, lighting his lamp and hucking it at the door. It
catches, and the Manse is now certainly _on fire_[^3]. At the very least, the Alzabo is
probably going to die in there before it manages to make a nuisance of itself.

# Magic Mirror on the Floor

Hastily, the party beats a retreat from the burgeoning blaze and continues on into the
Manse. Through the large double doors in the foyer, they find themselves in a large domed
room littered with shards of glass and the ragged remains of torn tapestrys. Inspecting
them, gingerly picking their way through the glass, they find a little more out about the
Manse's history and some of the Cyclopean legends. They also find a tapestry mostly
intact that appears to depict the Eye of Aeons - a huge glowing jewel radiating magic.

Everyone has been avoiding stepping into a large white circle in the middle of the room.
**Greg** decides to risk it, and everyone holds their breath as he steps over the
boundary. He seems unharmed and unfazed, so begins to lift the tapestry piled in the
centre to reveal a mirror lying on the floor.

**Lola** joins him, and reaches out a black-gloved hand to touch the surface. As she
does, it ripples alarmingly and she wrenches her arm back and free from the force
dragging her inside.

That's weird, well, only one solution to this for **Greg**: better piss on it.

The mirror doesn't really react to being pissed on, aside from just impercitbely a voice
hisses, "oh, what the fuck?".

**Bungle** flips a silver coin onto the mirror as well, but nothing happens again. It
seems the mirror only reacts to living beings. Luckily **Stickman** has just such a
living being trapped in one of his glass jars. He uncorks the stopper, a string of
muffled expletives suddenly become much louder, and hucks the gremlin out into the middle
of the mirror, the expletives stop for a moment, "Ooh, a penny".

As the gremlin is sucked into the mirror, and falls to the ground inside, the expletives
resume (slightly tinny through the glass). After a moment though, the colour drains from
the gremlins face and his eyes widen in abject terror. As he turns away from the mirror,
the party see a slender insect-like woman's hand reach out from where they can't see and
snatch the pitiful creature out of sight.

There is the unmistakable sickening crunch of a small snapped neck.

# Lack of Faith

The party decides to heft the mirror up and carry it with them, and move off through the
east door. Here, they find a long corridor with a door directly ahead of them and one to
the north. Heading northward they find themselves in another dormitory block.

There might be supplies here, so they gingerly put the mirror down and set-to turning the
place upside down. As they're doing so **Bungle** hears a quiet fizzing pop from one of
the closed doors. He gingerly approaches, and listens at the door.

"Get off me you Oaf!"

"I can't, Falx is on me I can barely move!"

"Here, get your elbow off my hat."

**Bungle** knocks tenatively, the voices stop with a sharp shushing hiss. After another
moment one of the voices rather gingerly says,

"Urhh, Who is it?"

And **Bungle** responds with a,

"Meow?"

The voices resume in a whisper,

"Did a cat just knock on the door?"

"There's no way! Trickery of the highest order!"

"Perhaps not my hastily assumptive friend, I had a cat once who could knock and ring
simple bells."

"What, in all the spheres names, is a _simple_ bell."

"A basic and normative one, you dolt, a basic string pulled bell."

"Hardly a great feat for a cat to yank a bell string."

"T'wouldn't be, save the creature could chime out the answers to high order mathmological
problems."

"It could not, you blustering baboon!"

**Bungle** opens the door to reveal three aged wizards in a heap on a bed, their argument
comes to an abrupt halt. They scramble up off the bed and the one with the largest hat
addresses the party,

"Well good gods, who the hell are you?"

Brief introductions, neither party seems to have much cause to come to blows immediately.
It seems that these wizards have been sent by Zoldar as well, and promised slightly more
money. Sensing a possible threat to their mission, the party promptly decides to shove
the wizards into the mirror lying on the ground.

They succeed with two of them, but the last one manages to loose a quick Flash and turn
on them with a cackle. A close quarters fight erupts between the party and the remaining
wizard, named Batón. Spells fly, blind shots from **Lola**'s fusil are recklessly fired
off, but eventually caught in a web from **Greg**'s nose and beaten quite harshly by
**Stickman**'s club, the wizard submits.

Having seen the wizard in action, the party now think it might have been a little hasty,
and perhaps some kind of agreement could be struck? They offer a share of their treasure
(which they categorically do not intend to honour) and to help him free his two friends.
Batón seems a little taken aback,

"Free them? Gods above no - they're up for the same tenure position as me! Keep 'em in
the mirror I say!"

And indeed, what's happening in the mirror? Looking inside, it seems that the two wizards
have resoundingly thrashed the fiend inside (they're _much_ more powerful than a single
gremlin) and are now shouting and fuming at everyone on the other side. The gang elects
to prop the mirror facing the wall and leave them here.

# Snails in the Stacks

Moving back to the main dome, with Batón in tow, the party decides to head south. They
enter another long corridor and stride confidently down it. About halfway, an obsidian
wall materialises across the corridor, blocking the door at the other end.

They turn expectantly to Batón,

"Well, looks to me to be some kind of magical wall."

The party is thoroughly unimpressed with this answer and approach the wall. As they do, a
face materialises in it and booms out,

"Speak the password, clearly and at a moderate volume."

They don't know a password (just yet, at any rate), but there are two doors to the east
and west of this corridor. **Bungle** shoulder barges through the east one, revealing the
boat house and docked boats, then does the same on the other side, revealing the library.

The library seems like a good place to start, so they head in. On an ornate desk in the
centre, surrounded by shelves crammed with books reaching all the way up to the domed
ceiling, is a large and demonic looking book chained to the desk.

Batón pushes everyone out the way,

"This is my specialty, everyone stand back and I shall uncover this book's mysteries."

The book looks quite cursed in many ways, so the party is quite happy to let him crack
on, while they investigate the rest of the room. They rummage around and find a number of
valuable looking historical treatises and a slightly less offensive seeming spellbook.

At the very top of the room, nestled in the rafters, one of **GBPAR11**'s hands is
scuttling around and disturbs a nest of mutated _Chaos Snails_. As they awaken, they
begin to squirm at snail speed down towards the party.

Everyone rushes over to Batón, who brushes them off,

"Stop crowding me, I'm not done yet!"

But the snails are, slowly but surely, bearing down on them. **GBPAR11** tries to melt
through the chain holding the book down, and **Bungle** brings his axe down on it and
breaks the chain with a mighty crack. The whole gang hot-foots it over to the boats
opposite the corridor, and launches out into the lake.

Still moving at snail speed, the snails skid to a halt on the jetty, one of them failing
to stop itself in time and falling with a splosh into the water.

# Snails in the Water

Out on the lake, the party punts out toward the fountain first. On the calm water, Batón
and **GBPAR11** manage to finish deciphering the two spell books. The demonic looking
book contains a "Summon Demon Wall" spell (so that's a mystery solved), but before the
party can do anything Batón rips out the spellbooks arch-page and swallows it[^4].
**GBPAR11** does the same with his book, learning the spell Jolt.

At the fountain now, **Greg** thinks the huge open fish mouths look quite inviting, and
as it's close enough to a pond, decides to dive into one of them. He finds it leads down
into a tunnel, though quickly becoming dark.

Luckily, **Lola** knows the spell Cone of Air, which will let the crew swim through
relatively easily. **Greg** goes first, with one of **GBPAR11**'s hands clutched onto his
shoulder. He swims through safely, eventually reaching a pitch dark dome filled with
refuse and detritus and a number of large chaos snails. He lurks out of sight in the
water, waiting for the rest of party to join him.

They do so, and then set about exterminating the 5 snails. **Bungle** goes on what can
only be described as a "spree", crushing snail shells with giant cleaving swings. In
short order, the snails are dispatched.

There are three doors to the north-west of this room. As the party are deciding on one to
open, from the right-most one a small swarm of eyes (Oculi) push through and float into
the room. The party is alarmed, but as the eyes seem to be doing nothing but watching
them, they press on, taking the middle door of the three.

# The Red Eye

In the large dome they enter, there are 6 doors. One catches their eyes immediately - a
huge ornate brass door emblazoned with eye imagery. Sensing their objective may be close
by, the party approach and push on it. It's locked, but **GBPAR11** manages to break open
the lock with their soldering iron (useful tool), and as the door begins to swing open
**Lola** rushes in to blast any occupants with a Flash.

Inside she sees, unfortunately, that plan is unlikely to work as the cyclops inside is
covering his single eye with a huge hand. He wields a large greatsword, and is bedecked
in ceremonial armour. Behind him, floating just above a huge altar, is _The Eye of
Aeons_, a huge glittering jewel throwing out refractions of impossible colour in every
direction.

The cyclops cries out, mournfully,

"Halt, foes of the Manse! Go no further, this accursed place holds nothing for the likes
of you!"

The gang is having none of it, and charge forward as the cyclops blows out a horn blast
of alarm. During a bitter fight, **Stickman** sneaks round to the altar as **Lola** and
**Bungle** are beaten half to death by the Cyclops. He swipes the huge jewel, stuffs it
in a sack and sprints for the door, shouting to his friends to book it as well.

As he does so, **Bungle** bravely stands in front of Cyclops, he is either nobly holding
the monster back from his friends or simply too bloody-minded to flee from the obvious
danger. The huge figure opens his eye, and lets out a blast of red energy, incinerating
**Bungle** forever, and the rest of the gang escape, **Bungle** forever in their hearts.

# A New Friend

And this brings us to the end of our tale[^5], the party flees. During the escape
**Lola** attempts to levitate **Stickman**, but fails and instead summons a healthy pig.
Is this the spirit of **Bungle** returned to us? We may never know. The camera zooms in
on the pig, who winks mischeivously at the viewer, before the credits roll.

[^1]: It should be barricaded but I forgot.

[^2]:
    Out of a possible surfeit of kindness from myself I decided to let them get a look in
    before just loosing the Alzabo on them - it's a pretty gnarly monster.

[^3]:
    I kind of subsequently forgot entirely about this and didn't really lean on it very
    much, which is a shame. It had been a very long day, in my defence.

[^4]:
    I decided, while running a Slate & Chalcedony session, that this is how spells and
    spellbooks work in my games. You have to physically eat a specific page from the
    spellbook (or scroll, or wherever the spell is written) to learn it.

    This works well for my games, I'm almost always running one-shots and making spells
    easy to learn is fun, but this limits it to not letting an entire party learn a spell
    they find with no cost. It's a good balance I feel.

[^5]:
    The bar staff were stacking chairs on tables at this point, it was 10pm so time to
    wrap up. I would have preferred to make them actually escape as well - but this was a
    pretty good place to stop.
